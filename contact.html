Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical turnaround time for a website project?

Standard business websites generally take between 2 to 4 weeks from discovery to final deployment. More complex platforms such as custom e-commerce stores or web applications typically take 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope.

Do you handle website deployment and hosting setup?

Yes. We handle end-to-end production deployment, including cPanel static or dynamic hosting setup, SSL certificate activation, domain configuration, and performance checks before launch.

Can I update my website content after it goes live?

Absolutely. If your project is built on a CMS like WordPress or Shopify, we provide training so your team can easily add new content, products, or blog posts independently.
